Danielle Mani - Complete Redesign

The new design follows a clean, editorial, minimalist-yet-modern aesthetic using your color palette (#7D0323 crimson, black, white).

Sections designed:
Navbar - Clean horizontal bar with "DANIELLE MANI" in Fraunces serif, navigation links in DM Sans, and a crimson "Contact" pill button

Hero Section - Split layout with bold serif headline ("Transformez votre corps, libérez votre esprit."), descriptive text, two CTAs (crimson primary + outlined secondary), and a dramatic dark fitness photo on the right

Mon Parcours (About) - Alternating layout with a black & white athletic image on the left, section label with crimson accent line, serif headline, body text about the BEMF diploma and background, and a black CTA button

Mon Approche (Services) - Centered section header with three equal-width cards, each with a photo, numbered label (01/02/03), Fraunces title, and description for La pratique physique, La nutrition, and Le massage

Articles Section - Dark background (#0A0A0A) with editorial layout: large featured article card on the left, two smaller horizontal article cards stacked on the right, crimson category labels and "Lire" links

Contact Section - Two-column layout with contact info (email, phone with Lucide icons) on the left and a clean form (Nom, Email, Sujet, Message) with crimson "Envoyer" button on the right

Footer - Dark background with brand column, Contact/Siret/Social columns separated by crimson accent lines, Instagram & Facebook icons, and copyright bar

Design system:
Typography: Fraunces (serif) for headlines, DM Sans for body text
Colors: #7D0323 crimson accents, black/white primary, gray (#666/#888) for secondary text
Style: Generous whitespace, pill-shaped buttons, 16px rounded corners, thin accent lines, editorial rhythm alternating light/dark sections
